# NarcoRisks – Intended purpose and regulatory limits (legacy maintenance release)

**Intended purpose:** an interactive, multilingual reference for selecting and organizing **general anesthesia-related risk topics** for professional information and education. The existing category selectors, presets, summary, copy/TXT export and text blocks remain in the legacy version.

**Not intended for:** patient-specific risk assessment or prediction, diagnosis, monitoring, therapy/anesthesia recommendations, an authoritative or complete list of a particular patient's disclosure requirements, replacing a real physician-patient discussion, documenting a clinical consultation/consent, or proving that consent was obtained. Do not enter real patient or identifiable health information.

**Legacy limitation:** the original procedural mappings, presets and default-selected documentation-like text blocks are still present and **have not been changed** in this maintenance patch. Neither a selection nor its generated text establishes which events actually occurred or which risks must be explained.

**Medical-device status:** the publisher does not claim a specific medical intended purpose within the meaning of Regulation (EU) 2017/745. Regulatory qualification must nevertheless be based on the complete intended purpose, real features, UI, instructions and promotional claims. This statement is not an official determination or a guarantee that medical-device law does not apply.

**Evidence and liability:** there is no guarantee of completeness, continuous scientific currentness or clinical suitability in an individual case. Legally mandatory liability remains unaffected. This update is not a clinical or legal approval of the underlying content.
